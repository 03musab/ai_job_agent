# JobSnap Luminous Glass — 32s Voiceover Script (Your Voice)

**Target length:** ~30 seconds (~64 words, relaxed confident founder pace — ~2.1 words/sec).  
**Tip:** Record in **one continuous take** in a quiet room (phone Voice Memos or Windows Sound Recorder works great). Don't worry about hitting exact milliseconds—pause naturally at the line breaks and `src/voice.py` will level your audio to `-15 LUFS` and clean up pauses.
