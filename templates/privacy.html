Data Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026 • AI JobSnap Self-Hosted Architecture

1. Local-First Data Storage

AI JobSnap is designed as a local-first application. All user profiles, resume uploads, search criteria, scraped job listings, and application status records are stored in your local SQLite database (`jobs.db`).

2. Resume Parsing & Processing

When you upload a PDF or Docx resume to your profile, the file is saved directly into your local machine’s `uploads/` directory. Text parsing and keyword extraction are performed locally using standard Python libraries.

3. Gmail OAuth Integration

If you enable automated email reports, AI JobSnap uses your own `credentials.json` file to request OAuth access to the Gmail API. Your OAuth refresh tokens (`token.pickle`) are stored strictly on your device.

4. Zero External Telemetry

AI JobSnap does not incorporate third-party analytics scripts, tracking pixels, or remote data collection services.
