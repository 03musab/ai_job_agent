Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026 · AI JobSnap (jobsnap.me)

Terms of Service · Back to Sign In

1. Overview

AI JobSnap (jobsnap.me and app.jobsnap.me) is a career intelligence, job search, and recruiting platform. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we handle Google user data, and how you can control or delete your data.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Name, verified email address, chosen username, workspace role (Job Seeker or Recruiter), and profile photo provided when you sign up with email or Google Sign-In.
  • Career & Workspace Data: Resumes you upload, extracted skills, target job search filters, saved job listings, application statuses, notes, and (for recruiters) company profiles and job postings.
  • Technical & Session Data: Essential authentication cookies required to keep you signed in securely. We do not use third-party advertising trackers or sell personal information.

3. Google User Data & API Scopes

When you choose to sign in or connect your Google account, AI JobSnap accesses only the minimum Google data required for the features you use:

  • Google Sign-In (openid, userinfo.email, userinfo.profile): Used to authenticate your account, verify your email address, and display your name and profile picture inside your workspace.
  • Google Calendar (optional): Used solely to create application deadline and interview reminders that you configure or request.
  • Gmail API (optional): Used solely to send job-alert digests or user-approved outreach emails that you explicitly configure or trigger. We never read unrelated personal emails or sell mailbox contents.

Google API Limited Use Disclosure: AI JobSnap's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres strictly to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. AI JobSnap does not use Google Workspace API data to develop, improve, or train generalized AI or machine learning models, and does not share Google user data with third-party data brokers or advertisers.

4. AI-Assisted Features

When you invoke on-demand AI features—such as resume-to-job compatibility scoring, skill-gap roadmaps, or job description formatting—only the relevant resume or job text needed to generate that response is processed. We do not permit advertising use of your career data.

5. Data Security, Retention & Deletion

All traffic to app.jobsnap.me is encrypted in transit via HTTPS/TLS, and account passwords are stored using strong one-way cryptographic hashes. OAuth tokens are stored securely solely to perform actions you authorize.

You may revoke AI JobSnap's Google permissions at any time from your Google Account Permissions page. To request deletion of your AI JobSnap account and all associated records, email support@jobsnap.me from your registered address and we will permanently delete your data within 7 days.

6. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or your data rights? Contact us at support@jobsnap.me.
